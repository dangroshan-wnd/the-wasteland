# Week 1 Changed The Flock League Forever

Week 1 concluded with wins for Tom, Mason, Danny, Badake, Austin, and Bush. Badake posted the league-high 148.30 points, while injuries to Puka Nacua and Christian McCaffrey materially affected Nathan and Thomas, respectively.

## Week 1 results

Tom beat Nathan 129.58–118.98; Mason beat Thomas 106.42–93.06; Danny beat Zach 141.36–119.46; Badake beat Joe 148.30–99.22; Austin beat Avery 141.82–124.48; and Bush beat Sal 118.84–106.58. Badake led the league in Week 1 scoring with 148.30 points.

## Injuries and roster moves

Puka Nacua was placed on injured reserve and later left Week 1 listed out; Nathan started him and received 8.20 points. Thomas had Christian McCaffrey inactive for Monday night. Tee Higgins was doubtful for Week 1 with a hamstring injury. Mason made free-agent moves before and after the week, including adding Isaac Guerendo and dropping Isaiah Davis.

## Trade talk

Several offers remained unresolved. An offer involving Kenneth Walker for Rhamondre Stevenson and Austin Ekeler was not accepted. Badake put Saquon Barkley and A.J. Brown on the trade block, while an offer sending Ja'Marr Chase for Calvin Ridley and DJ Moore was shown as unaccepted. Mason also offered Rhamondre Stevenson and Caleb Williams for James Cook.
