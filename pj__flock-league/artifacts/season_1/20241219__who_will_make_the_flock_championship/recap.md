# Who Will Make The Flock Championship?

Week 16 playoff previews centered on the confirmed semifinals of Zach vs. Avery and Mason vs. Thomas, with Zach and Thomas narrowly favored by Sleeper projections. The Toilet Bowl field was narrowed in discussion to Nathan, Bush, and Badake, with the week’s highest scorer among them escaping T-shirt-punishment contention. No Week 16 matchup results were confirmed in this episode.

## Playoff semifinals

Zach and Avery were set for a Week 16 semifinal, projected 142.69–139.69 in Zach’s favor (52%–48%). Mason and Thomas were the other repeatedly stated semifinal, projected 129.83–125.78 for Thomas (52%–48%). Thomas had advanced over Austin in a close matchup the prior week. Commentary also disputed the league’s non-reseeding playoff setup, but no official bracket change was shown.

## Roster and injury watch

Thomas was reported to be without David Montgomery, who was said to have torn his MCL, and Alvin Kamara was also discussed as unavailable/uncertain. Jerome Ford was mentioned as a possible Thomas pickup. Zach added Sincere McCormick as a free agent and was said to have previously acquired Mike Evans. Kenneth Walker’s availability was a concern in the Zach–Avery matchup, while Avery expected Aidan O’Connell to return, benefiting Brock Bowers and Jakobi Meyers.

## Toilet Bowl

Nathan, Bush, and Badake were identified as the three remaining Toilet Bowl contenders. The highest Week 16 scorer among the three would be safe from the punishment T-shirt. Nathan vs. Steven was projected essentially even at 129.32–128.97, while Badake was favored over Bush, 128.38–123.71. Nathan discussed Tony Pollard’s questionable status and a possible switch from Josh Downs to Rachaad White.

## Predictions, not results

Participants predicted a Zach–Mason championship and a Bush–Badake Toilet Bowl final if Nathan posted the highest score. These were opinions and projections only; neither finalist nor any Week 16 result was confirmed.
